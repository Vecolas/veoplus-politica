# Veo+ Terms of Service

**Last updated:** November 25, 2026

*Versão em português: [Termos de Uso](termos.html).*

Veo+ is a desktop application by Vecola Productions that produces short videos
on the user's computer and, when authorized, uploads them to the user's own
YouTube channel.

## 1. What the application does

From a topic, it writes the script with the AI assistant of the user's own
account, narrates it with the user's voice, builds 3D scenes, creates the
thumbnail and uploads the video to the authorized channel, in Portuguese and
English when the user sets up two profiles.

## 2. What the application does not do

- **It does not publish without the user's decision.** The default mode uploads
  the video as private for review in YouTube Studio. Immediate publishing must
  be chosen and confirmed by the user.
- **It does not hide the use of AI.** Every upload is marked as altered or
  synthetic content, with no option to turn it off.
- **It does not circumvent YouTube limits, protections or terms.** There is no
  automation of views, subscriptions or interactions, and no access to other
  people's channels.

## 3. User responsibilities

By using Veo+, you declare that you:

1. use only your own voice, or the voice of someone who authorized it in
   writing;
2. are responsible for the content you publish, including its accuracy and
   compliance with the [YouTube Community Guidelines][guidelines];
3. review the video before making it public: the application prepares, it does
   not approve.
